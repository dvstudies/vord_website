### Visual Open Research Data Workshop

**Friday, 11 December 2026, 9:30–15:00** 

University of Zurich, KO2-F-174  

*Open to the public and streamed online*


How do scholars think with images, and how can we meaningfully compare these practices with the rapidly expanding visual capabilities of multimodal AI?

The concluding public workshop of the VORD2 Visual Open Research Data project presents the results of a series of experimental *Thinking with Images* sessions in which researchers made explicit how images generate questions, support interpretation, resist classification, and redirect scholarly inquiry.

Presentations and demos will connect qualitative analysis of image-based epistemic practices with computational work on visual reasoning, segmentation, retrieval and benchmarking. An afternoon roundtable will bring together scholars from art history, visual studies, digital humanities, AI and research infrastructures to discuss how future open research tools might support, and not flatten, the plurality, ambiguity and situated character of visual knowledge.

The workshop concludes VORD2 while opening its next phase towards shared frameworks and open resources for studying how humans and machines think with images.
