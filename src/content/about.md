# About

VORD is a research initiative based at Digital Visual Studies, University of Zurich. The project team includes researchers, designers, and software developers working at the intersection of art history, data science, and digital humanities infrastructure.

## Team

The initiative is led by researchers at Digital Visual Studies with contributions from collaborators at partner institutions across Europe. We work iteratively and in public, sharing our process alongside our results.

## Contact

For inquiries about the project, collaboration proposals, or data access requests, please reach out through the Digital Visual Studies website at dvs.uzh.ch.

## Acknowledgements

VORD has received support from the University of Zurich's Digital Transformation Initiative and benefits from the infrastructure maintained by Digital Visual Studies. We acknowledge the contributions of partner institutions and the open-source communities whose tools underpin our work.

## Citing VORD

If you use VORD data or tools in your research, please cite the specific dataset or software release. Citation information is provided with each published resource in our repository.
