# Mission

VORD — Visual Open Research Data — is an initiative of Digital Visual Studies at the University of Zurich dedicated to making visual research data openly accessible, reproducible, and reusable across disciplines.

We believe that images, diagrams, maps, and visual datasets are primary carriers of knowledge that have been systematically underrepresented in open science infrastructures. VORD addresses this gap by developing standards, tools, and workflows for publishing structured visual data alongside the scholarship that produces it.

Our work spans three interrelated domains: the curation of visual corpora from cultural heritage institutions; the development of annotation and metadata frameworks grounded in art historical practice; and the design of open publication formats that enable downstream computational analysis.

We are committed to working in the open — sharing datasets, code, documentation, and research in progress through public repositories and collaborative platforms. All outputs produced under the VORD initiative are released under open licenses.

VORD is embedded within Digital Visual Studies at the University of Zurich and maintains active collaborations with museums, libraries, and research groups across Europe and beyond.
