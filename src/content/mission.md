title: Mission
---

VORD, in its conception, aims at apparently very technical objectives. We build open benchmarks for vision-language models (VLMs), fine-tune small models on art-historical tasks, and develop a chatbot for the visual humanities. But while working on these we have constated that current VLMs are epistemologically impoverished from a humanities perspective. while they excel at description, captioning, and rudimentary comparison, they have no explicit model of how images function as tools of thought because they lack an epistemology of the image that includes the humanities and considers the actual research practices carefully. 

If we want benchmarks, and eventually AI tools, that respond to genuine scholarly practice, we first need to understand that practice in vivo rather than trough surveys. Surveys demand that respondents already know how they think, and force pre-categorization into a matrix of "methods" (formal, iconographic, psychoanalytic, decolonial…). Taxonomy presumes that thinking with images is a species of applied theory while It usually is not. Research practices hides more interesting and noisy details that are worth looking into.
