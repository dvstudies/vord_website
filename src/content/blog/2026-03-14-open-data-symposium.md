In March, members of the VORD team participated in a broader symposium on open data practices in cultural heritage institutions, hosted at the Kunsthaus Zürich.

The event brought together museum professionals, digital archivists, and researchers to discuss the practical and institutional challenges of releasing collection data under open licenses.

VORD contributed a session on the specific requirements of visual datasets — arguing that existing open data frameworks, largely designed for tabular or textual data, need significant extension to accommodate the spatial, relational, and interpretive dimensions of image data.

The discussion surfaced important questions about granularity: at what level should visual data be described? How do we balance the desire for fine-grained annotation with the realities of curatorial bandwidth?

These questions continue to inform our schema development. A summary of the session will be published as part of the symposium proceedings.
