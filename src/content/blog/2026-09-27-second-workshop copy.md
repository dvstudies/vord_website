The first VORD workshop brought together researchers, curators, and digital humanists from across Europe to explore shared challenges in visual data publication.

Over two days at the University of Zurich, participants examined existing open data infrastructures, discussed annotation standards for visual corpora, and tested early prototypes of VORD's curation toolchain.

Sessions ranged from hands-on demonstrations of dataset preparation workflows to theoretical discussions about what it means for visual knowledge to be open. A recurring theme was the tension between the richness of visual description and the demands of machine-readable formats.

The workshop concluded with a set of working principles for the VORD metadata schema and a roadmap for the first public data release, planned for later this year.

We are grateful to all participants for their generosity in sharing expertise and their willingness to engage critically with our proposals. Documentation from the workshop — including session notes, slides, and preliminary schema drafts — will be made available in the coming weeks.
